📂 Overview

Adobe Photoshop requires a scratch disk to store temporary data while you edit large images. When your primary drive reaches capacity, Photoshop displays a scratch disk full error. This prevents the program from starting or saving your work. This tool automates the process of finding and clearing these temporary files to restore performance on Windows 10 and 11.

⚙️ System Requirements

  • Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11.
  • Software: Adobe Photoshop installed on your local drive.
  • Storage: Administrator permissions to clear temporary system folders.
